01 / A small apology♡

Abe sun na,

Ek baar poora padh ke dekh. Pehle maine tere saath jo bhi mazaak kiye ya jo bhi bola, usse tujhe hurt hua ho ya bura laga ho, toh I'm genuinely sorry. Mazaak keh dene se koi baat chhoti nahi ho jaati. Mujhe apne words ka dhyaan rakhna chahiye tha.

Bas ek baat kehni thi — shayad tu mere baare mein jaisa sochti hai, main apni taraf se waisa nahi hoon. Lekin main samajhta hoon ki sirf mere kehne se tera nazariya nahi badlega. Meri baaton aur actions ne tujhe jo feel karaya, woh bhi matter karta hai.

Rahi Krishna ke account ki baat, haan, maine password guess kiya tha aur woh sahi nikla. Bina permission account kholna meri galti thi. Wahan jo personal chats aur photos the, woh tum dono ki private baatein thi. Unki details yahan laana ya unhe explain karke apni galti justify karna sahi nahi hoga. Uske liye bhi sorry.

Aur agar tujhe lagta hai ki maine tera mazaak udaya, toh main itna kehna chahta hoon ki tujhe neecha dikhana mera intention nahi tha. Phir bhi, meri kisi baat se tujhe aisa feel hua, toh main usse ignore nahi karna chahta.

Tujhse reply ya maaf karne ki koi expectation nahi hai. Tu jo bhi decide kare, main respect karunga. Bas apni taraf se dil se sorry kehna tha.

Bas, itna hi kehna tha. ♡

No pressure to reply. Take your time.
